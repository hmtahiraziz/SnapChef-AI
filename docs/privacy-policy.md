# Privacy Policy — SnapChef AI

**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SnapChef AI (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the SnapChef AI mobile application.

Host this page at a public HTTPS URL (for example GitHub Pages) and set `EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` to that URL for Google Play Console and the in-app “View online version” link.

## Who we are

SnapChef AI helps you turn available ingredients into cookable recipes using on-device capture and cloud AI services.

## Information we collect

- **Account data** — name, email, and profile photo managed through Clerk authentication.
- **Ingredient photos** — images you choose to capture or upload for ingredient scanning.
- **Preferences** — cuisine/country and appearance settings.
- **App content you create** — favorites and shopping-list items.
- **Technical data** — information needed to operate and support the app (for example app version).

## How we use information

We use this information to:

- Create and secure your account
- Extract ingredients from photos and generate recipes
- Sync favorites when you are signed in
- Provide support and improve reliability

Photos submitted for scanning are processed to return ingredient suggestions. Processing is performed by our AI provider under their terms.

## Third-party services

We use:

- **Clerk** — authentication and account management
- **Our API host** — backend API
- **MongoDB** — favorites storage when signed in
- **OpenAI** — vision and recipe generation

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to scan ingredients or update your profile photo. We do not record audio.

## Retention and deletion

You can sign out at any time. You can delete your account in the app under **Settings → Account → Delete account**. Deleting your account removes your Clerk account and stops cloud sync of favorites for that account. Local data on your device may remain until you clear app storage.

## Your choices

- Decline camera or library permissions (you can still enter ingredients as text)
- Update profile details in Account
- Delete your account in-app as described above

## Children

SnapChef AI is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the SnapChef AI store listing.
